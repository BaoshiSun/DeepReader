DeepReader for iPhone and iPad is a free native reader for local PDF, EPUB, TXT and Markdown. No DeepReader account, subscription, ads, bookstore or catalog is required. Local reading, search, ratings and highlights work without an API key. EPUB is text-only and Markdown displays source. DRM, MOBI/AZW3, OCR and sync are unsupported.

Testing: In Library, tap Read a sample (打开示例文章), or Import from Files. In Settings, toggle English if needed. Touch and hold text to select it; the floating assistant opens without sending anything. Drag the title to move it; minus collapses it, X closes it. Tap Explain. First-use disclosure covers selections/context, follow-ups and full-text summaries; Allow, remember and send stores permission locally. Settings → Data and privacy can revoke it. Subsequent user-initiated requests do not repeat consent. Closing the disclosure without approving sends nothing.

Only DeepSeek is supported. Users provide their own key in Settings, stored in iOS Keychain. Provider fees and quotas are separate. AI requests go directly to api.deepseek.com. Follow-up adds the prior answer and question; Summary sends extracted full text in sections. Successful responses appear in History; records can be deleted by swiping.

Imported documents are private local copies, preserving originals. Long-press a library card to remove its copy. PDF highlights persist in the imported copy and can be exported using the reader menu. Data, privacy and source/license information are in Settings. Review-only provider access remains in the private App Review Notes below; it is not bundled in the app or public source.
